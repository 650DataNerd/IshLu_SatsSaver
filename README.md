# IshLu — Bitcoin-Backed Loans for Africa

Don't sell your Bitcoin. Get a loan.

## What is IshLu?

IshLu lets Bitcoin holders in Africa unlock liquidity without selling their BTC. Lock your Bitcoin as collateral, receive Kenyan Shillings instantly via M-Pesa, repay the loan and get your Bitcoin back.

## How it works

1. Deposit Bitcoin via Lightning Network
2. Your BTC is locked as collateral
3. Receive KES instantly via M-Pesa
4. Repay the loan to unlock your Bitcoin

## Loan Terms

- Loan-to-Value: 67%
- Annual Rate: 11.49%
- Terms: 30, 90, 180, or 365 days
- Minimum collateral: 100,000 sats
- Disbursement: M-Pesa (Kenya)

## Trust Score

IshLu builds Africa's first Bitcoin credit score based on loan repayment history. Start at 400 (Seed). Repay on time to unlock higher loan limits.

Private beta. Built in Nairobi.
